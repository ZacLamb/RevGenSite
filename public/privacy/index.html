Privacy

Privacy, in plain English

What Revenue Generating Solutions collects on this website, and why.

Website visits

We count visits so we know which pages people read. To do that, this site sets one small first-party cookie containing a random ID, which lets us recognize a repeat visit. We record the pages viewed, the time, the general type of device (phone, tablet or computer) and how you arrived, such as a Google search or a link from another site. We do not store your IP address and we don't run third-party advertising or analytics scripts on these pages.

When you contact us

If you send us the contact form, we keep what you give us (name, business, email, phone and your message) so we can reply. After you've sent it, later visits from the same browser may be linked to your request, so we can see you're still interested and follow up promptly. We also note which source brought you to the site, such as Google or a referral, to see what's working.

Booking a call

Our booking calendar is provided by GoHighLevel and loads inside the page. What you enter there goes to our CRM. On our own site we only note that someone clicked a button to book, not who they are.

Sharing

We don't sell your information. Contact requests are stored in our CRM (GoHighLevel) so our team can respond.

Questions or removal

To ask about or remove your information, email hello@revenuegeneratingsolutions.com or call (617) 877-5608. You can also clear this site's cookies in your browser at any time.
