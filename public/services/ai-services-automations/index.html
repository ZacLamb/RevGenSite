FAQ

Common questions

Will an AI agent sound robotic to my customers?

No — every voice and chat agent is scripted and trained on your business, your tone, and your services before it ever talks to a real lead. Most callers don't realize they're not talking to a person until you tell them.

What if I lose a lead because the AI can't answer something?

Every automation we build has a clear handoff path — a live transfer, an instant text alert to your phone, or a booked callback — so nothing falls through a gap.

Do I need a CRM already in place?

No. We can build the automation layer on top of your existing CRM or set one up for you as part of the project — see CRM Build & Management.
