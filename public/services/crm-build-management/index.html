FAQ

Common questions

We already have a CRM. Can you just fix it?

Yes — a lot of our CRM work is cleanup and rebuild on systems that were set up once and never touched again. We'll audit what you have before recommending a rebuild.

Do you only work with GoHighLevel?

GoHighLevel is our primary platform because of what it can automate in one place, but we've built and managed CRMs on other platforms too — we'll recommend what fits your business.

Is ongoing management required?

No — we offer one-time build-outs and monthly management separately. Most clients start with a build and add management once they see the volume it creates.
