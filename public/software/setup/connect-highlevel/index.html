How to set up RGS Tools and connect HighLevel

Create your RGS Tools account and connect your GoHighLevel agency in one step, or connect a single sub-account with a Private Integration token.

Start a 7-day free trial Full written setup guide
Step by step

Create your account and connect HighLevel

  1. Go to app.revenuegeneratingsolutions.com and click Start free trial. Sign up with your work email.
  2. Open Sub-accounts and click Connect.
  3. Running an agency? Choose Whole agency. It installs the RGS Tools app from the HighLevel Marketplace: pick your agency (not a single sub-account) and approve the permissions.
  4. Tick the sub-accounts you want the tools on and click Connect. Add more any time.
  5. Only one sub-account, or no agency access? Choose One sub-account, create a Private Integration in HighLevel with the scopes listed in the Connect window, then paste the token and the Location ID.
  6. Your sub-accounts now show in the switcher at the top left of every tool.
