FAQ

Questions about the AI audit

What does the free AI audit cost?

Nothing. It is a 30-minute call. If we can help, we will tell you what we would build and what it would cost. If we cannot, we will say so.

Do I need to be technical?

No. We ask how your business runs today: how customers reach you, what your team does every day and which tools you already use. We do the technical part.

What kind of businesses is this for?

Small and growing local businesses of any kind: home services, clinics, restaurants, salons, shops, offices and more, anywhere in New England.

Will AI replace my staff?

That is rarely the goal. Most owners use it to stop missing calls and leads and to give their team back the hours they spend on repetitive work.

What happens to my customer data?

We build on tools you control and set clear rules for what AI can see and do. Nothing is shared or used to train public models without your say-so.

Do you only work in Massachusetts?

No. We work with businesses in all six New England states. Most of the work is remote, with on-site visits when they help.
