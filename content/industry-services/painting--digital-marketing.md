## The problem in painting

A painting company's marketing problem is mostly a timing problem. From late spring into early fall, the phone rings on its own and the real issue is getting to every estimate. From late fall through winter, exterior work disappears and crews need interior jobs to stay busy. Most painters turn ads on when it's slow and off when it's busy, which means they're always reacting a month late.

Other common leaks:

- **Ads that don't separate the work.** One campaign for "painters near me" sends exterior searchers, cabinet painting shoppers and commercial property managers to the same homepage.
- **Paying for towns you don't want.** A service radius that includes a city forty minutes away fills your calendar with long drives and lowball shoppers.
- **A Google Business Profile that sits still.** No new photos since the year you opened, a handful of reviews, and services listed as just "painting."
- **No idea which leads turned into jobs.** You know you spent money on ads. You don't know whether that $9,000 exterior came from Google, Facebook or a yard sign.

## What we run for painting companies

1. **Google Ads split by job type.** Separate campaigns or ad groups for exterior house painting, interior painting, cabinet painting, deck staining and commercial painting, each pointed at its own landing page and its own list of target towns.
2. **Local Services Ads setup and management.** As of 2026, painters can run Google Local Services Ads in many markets, where you pay for leads rather than clicks. We handle the profile, service selection and lead rating, and connect leads to your CRM.
3. **Meta campaigns that show the work.** Before-and-after carousels and short videos of exterior transformations shown to homeowners in your towns, plus off-season offers for interiors and cabinets.
4. **A seasonal budget calendar.** Spend shifts toward exterior terms ahead of spring, toward interior and commercial terms in the fall, and toward early-booking offers for next season's exteriors over the winter.
5. **Google Business Profile and review growth.** Accurate services, regular project photos tagged by town, and a steady review request process after each completed job.
6. **Town pages and content that build over time.** Location pages and helpful articles that keep bringing in search traffic after the ad budget stops.
7. **Monthly reporting tied to booked jobs.** Leads, estimates and won jobs by source, so you can see which campaigns paid for themselves.

## How it works

1. **Audit.** We review your current ad accounts, website and Google Business Profile to find the quickest fixes.

3. **Launch and track.** We connect every campaign to your CRM so each lead is tracked from click to signed proposal.
4. **Optimize monthly.** We cut what isn't producing estimates and move money toward what is, adjusting for the season.

## What to look for (even if you don't hire us)

Whether you run ads yourself or hire someone, check these:

- Are exterior, interior, cabinet and commercial searches in separate campaigns with separate landing pages?
- Is your target area drawn around the towns you actually want to work in, not just a radius?
- Are negative keywords blocking "painting classes," "paint store," "painter jobs" and art searches?
- Does the budget change with the season, or is it the same number every month?
- Can you see won jobs by lead source, not just clicks and impressions?
- How fast is someone calling back new leads? If it's hours, fix that before spending more. Our piece on [speed to lead](/blog/speed-to-lead-home-services/) explains why.
