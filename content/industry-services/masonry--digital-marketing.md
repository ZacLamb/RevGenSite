## The problem in masonry

Ask a mason where their best jobs came from and the answer is usually referrals, a yard sign, or a neighbor who watched the crew work. Those are great, but you can't turn them up in a slow April. When masons try paid ads, a few problems show up fast.

The first is lead quality. A broad "masonry contractor" campaign pulls in everyone: the person who wants one cracked step patched for $200, the landlord asking about a foundation crack you don't touch, the DIYer who wants advice. Meanwhile the homeowner planning a full paver patio with a seat wall searched "paver patio installer" plus their town and found a competitor whose Google Business Profile was stacked with project photos.

The second is timing. Masonry runs on weather. Ads that switch on when the ground thaws are already late, because buyers have been collecting quotes since February. Chimney work has its own rhythm before heating season.

The third is tracking. Big jobs close weeks after the first click, so if your ads report "conversions" as form fills, you have no idea which campaign produced the $30,000 wall and which produced nothing but tire kickers.

## What we build for masonry contractors

- **Google Ads split by job type.** Separate campaigns or ad groups for patios and walkways, retaining walls, chimney repair and rebuilds, and steps, each sending clicks to the matching service page, with negative keywords for work you don't take.
- **Local Services Ads where available.** Google's pay-per-lead ads cover a set list of home service categories that varies by market. As of 2026, check inside the signup whether your category is offered in your area before planning around it. Our comparison of [Local Services Ads and regular Google Ads](/blog/google-local-services-ads-vs-google-ads/) explains the trade-offs.
- **Meta campaigns built on finished projects.** Facebook and Instagram ads using your own before-and-after photos to reach homeowners in your towns who are likely planning outdoor work.
- **Google Business Profile work.** Accurate service categories, regular project photo posts tagged by town, and a steady review request process after each completed job.
- **Programmatic town and service pages** so organic search keeps sending leads after ad budgets pause for winter.
- **Monthly reporting tied to signed jobs.** Leads tracked from the first click into your CRM, so the report shows estimates and deposits by campaign, not just impressions.

## How it works

1. **Audit.** We look at your current ad accounts, your Business Profile, your website and where your existing leads actually come from.
2. **Build campaigns.** We set up or rebuild campaigns around your highest-margin work and your real service area, timed ahead of your season.
3. **Launch and track.** Every campaign connects to your CRM so a lead can be followed from click to estimate to signed contract.
4. **Optimize monthly.** We cut what produces junk, put more behind what produces signed jobs, and shift spend with the seasons.

## What to look for (even if you don't hire us)

- Will the agency show you search terms that triggered your ads, so you can see whether you are paying for "how to fix brick steps myself"?
- Are campaigns separated by the type of work, with each one landing on a matching page?
- Do they plan around your season, or run the same budget all year?
- Do reports show booked site visits and signed jobs, or only clicks and form fills?
- Will they post your own project photos, or use stock images of someone else's patio?
- Do you own the ad accounts and Business Profile outright?
