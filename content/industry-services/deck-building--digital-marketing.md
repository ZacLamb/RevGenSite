## The problem in deck building

For deck builders, demand is uneven and buyers are slow. Spring brings more inquiries than you can handle, often from people who want a finished deck in four weeks. Fall can be a great time to build, but the phone gets quieter, and winter is quieter still. Marketing that runs the same way all year spends money when you are already booked and goes silent when you need work.

The buyers themselves take time. A homeowner weighing a 400-square-foot composite deck with lighting and a stair landing might research for months before requesting a consultation. Many first see the idea on Facebook or Instagram, not in a Google search. By the time they search "deck builder near me," they often have a short list from what they already saw.

Your ads also compete with your own calendar. In April, a campaign that keeps generating consultation requests you can't get to until July mostly produces frustrated homeowners who sign with someone else. In October, the same campaign might be exactly what keeps a crew working until the ground freezes. Budgets need to move with your backlog, not with the month.

Then there is measuring what works. A deck lead might take weeks to turn into a deposit. If the agency reports clicks and form fills, you can't tell which campaign produced the composite deck with a pergola and which produced a stream of "can you fix a few loose boards?" requests.

## What we build for deck builders

- **Meta campaigns built around your finished decks.** Carousels and short videos of projects by material and style, aimed at homeowners in your service area, with a consultation offer. For how this compares with search, see [Google Ads vs. Meta ads for home services](/blog/google-ads-vs-meta-ads-home-services/).
- **Google search campaigns for high-intent terms.** Separate ad groups for new decks, composite decks, deck replacement and porches, each pointed at its own page, with negatives for small repairs if you don't take them.
- **Shoulder-season campaigns.** Fall build offers and winter "plan now, build in spring" consultation pushes to even out the year.
- **Google Business Profile work.** Project photos posted regularly with towns and materials, accurate service categories, and review requests after each final inspection.
- **Programmatic town and material pages** that keep bringing in organic traffic between campaigns.
- **Reporting tied to deposits.** Leads tracked into the CRM so reports show consultations, proposals and deposits by campaign.

## How it works

1. **Audit.** We review your ad accounts, Business Profile, site and recent sales to see what has worked and where money leaks.
2. **Build campaigns.** We build Meta and Google campaigns around your best work and your towns, with a calendar for each season.
3. **Launch and track.** Every campaign connects to your CRM so a lead can be followed from ad to consultation to deposit.
4. **Optimize monthly.** We report on deposits by campaign, cut the losers, and shift spend as your schedule fills or opens up.

## What to look for (even if you don't hire us)

- Will the agency use your actual project photos and video, or stock decks?
- Is there a plan for fall and winter, not just spring?
- Are search campaigns split by job type with matching landing pages?
- Do reports follow leads to consultations and deposits, not just clicks?
- Will they slow spending when your spring calendar is full?
- Do you own the ad accounts and the Business Profile outright?
