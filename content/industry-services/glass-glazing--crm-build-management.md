## The problem in glass and glazing

A glass shop is really running three businesses through one front counter. There's emergency service: board-ups and temporary glass after break-ins, accidents and storms. There's residential work: shower enclosures, mirrors, foggy insulated units, table tops, window and door glass. And there's commercial work: storefronts, entrance doors, office partitions and bids for general contractors.

In many shops, all three live in the same place: a phone, a notepad by the register, and an estimator's email inbox. That causes predictable losses. A board-up job gets done and invoiced, but nobody follows up to quote the permanent replacement, so the property owner calls someone else. A homeowner's shower door quote goes out and is never checked on. A commercial bid due Friday gets buried under a week of small residential calls.

Glass jobs also wait on things. A measure has to be booked. Tempered and custom glass gets ordered and can take a while to come in. Then the install gets scheduled, and the customer calls asking whether their glass is in yet. Without a system tracking those steps, someone in the office spends the day answering status questions.

## What we build for glass and glazing shops

- **Three pipelines.** An emergency pipeline (call in, dispatched, secured, replacement quote), a residential pipeline (inquiry, measure booked, quote sent, deposit, glass ordered, glass received, installed), and a commercial pipeline (bid request, takeoff, bid submitted, follow-up, awarded, submittals, ordered, installed).
- **Emergency-to-replacement handoff.** When a board-up is marked secured, the CRM automatically opens a replacement quote and schedules a follow-up so the permanent job isn't lost.
- **Glass order tracking.** Ordered and received stages with expected dates, and automatic "your glass is in, let's schedule" messages to customers.
- **Measure and install booking** with confirmations and reminders, plus rescheduling when a supplier delay pushes a date.
- **Quote follow-up** on residential jobs, and bid-deadline reminders and follow-up tasks on commercial bids. Our post on [speed to lead for home services](/blog/speed-to-lead-home-services/) explains why the first response matters so much.
- **One team inbox** for calls, texts and forms, with photos, dimensions and drawings stored on each record.
- **Monthly management** to keep the pipelines accurate and adjust them as your mix of work shifts.

## How it works

1. **Map your process.** We trace a recent emergency call, a shower door job and a commercial bid from first contact to final invoice.

3. **Migrate and train.** We bring in your existing customers, open quotes and bids, then train the counter staff, estimators and techs.
4. **Manage month to month.** We keep it clean and adjust it as you add services or staff.

- Can it run separate pipelines for emergency, residential and commercial work?
- Can you store measurements, photos and glass specs on the job record?
- Does it track glass on order and remind you when it's due in?
- Does it automatically start a replacement quote after an emergency board-up?
- Can it hold commercial contacts like property managers and contractors who send you repeat work?
- Will the vendor help keep it clean, or leave you with a messy system in six months?
