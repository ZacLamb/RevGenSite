## The problem in glass and glazing

Glass work arrives at two very different speeds. Some calls are emergencies: a storefront window shattered in a break-in, a sliding door broken by a stray ball, a car through the front of a shop, storm damage. Those callers need someone on the phone right now and a board-up or temporary glass within hours. Whoever answers first usually gets the job, and often the permanent replacement that follows.

Other calls are planned work with a long tail: a frameless shower enclosure, a set of insulated glass units with failed seals, mirrors for a gym, a commercial storefront bid from a general contractor. These need measurements, photos, glass specs and a quote, and many of them stall because nobody followed up after the quote went out.

Small and mid-size glass shops usually have a handful of people covering the phone, the counter, the cutting table and the van. After hours, calls roll to a cell phone or voicemail. During the day, a tech on a ladder can't stop to ask a caller for the width of their shower opening. Emergencies wait in voicemail, and quote requests sit half-collected.

## What we build for glass and glazing shops

- **An AI voice and chat agent with emergency triage.** It answers every call and chat, asks a few safety and urgency questions, and routes true emergencies to your on-call tech by live transfer or instant text with the address and details. Non-urgent calls get booked or logged. Our guide to [AI receptionists for contractors](/blog/ai-receptionist-for-contractors/) covers how to set the rules.
- **Residential and commercial routing.** The agent recognizes property managers, building owners and contractors and sends them to your commercial estimator, while homeowners get the residential path.
- **Photo and measurement collection.** Automated texts after a quote request asking for photos of the opening, rough dimensions, glass type and hardware finish, attached to the lead record.
- **Quote documents drafted for you.** Quotes generated from the details collected and your price sheet, ready for your review before they go out.
- **Quote follow-up sequences** for shower doors, windows and mirrors, plus a slower, more personal cadence for commercial bids.
- **Appointment reminders** for measure visits and installs, and review requests after completed jobs.

## How it works

1. **Audit.** We look at your call log, after-hours handling and quote process to see where emergencies and estimates slip.
2. **Design.** We script the agent around your rules: what counts as an emergency, who is on call, which jobs you take and which you refer.
3. **Build and connect.** We connect it to your phones, CRM and calendar so details land on the right record and the right person gets alerted.
4. **Tune and support.** We monitor calls and transcripts closely for the first 60 days, then keep adjusting as your service mix changes.

## What to look for (even if you don't hire us)

- Can the agent tell a true emergency from a routine call and alert a real person instantly?
- Can it route commercial and residential callers differently?
- Does it collect photos and dimensions in a way your estimator can actually use?
- Can you review every call recording and transcript?
- What happens if your on-call tech doesn't respond to the alert? There should be a backup.
- Are follow-up texts sent from a registered number? As of 2026, US business texting generally requires A2P 10DLC registration.
