## The problem in electrical

Electrical work runs from a quick troubleshooting call to a full service upgrade with a new meter socket and panel, and most homeowners don't know the difference when they search. "Electrician near me" brings in both. If your campaigns treat every lead the same, the small calls eat the budget and the office spends its day quoting outlet swaps.

The high-margin work has its own demand patterns. EV charger installs follow car purchases. Standby generators get searched after a big storm knocks out power for a few days. Panel upgrades come up when someone is adding a heat pump, finishing a basement, or selling an older house with a fuse box. Campaigns that sit unchanged all year miss those moments.

Common problems we see in electricians' accounts:

- One campaign for everything, sending traffic to the homepage
- No separation between residential, commercial and emergency searches
- Ads showing in towns outside your license area or past where your crews will drive
- No record of which leads became signed jobs, so nobody knows the real cost per job
- A Google Business Profile listing "Electrician" with no services, photos or recent reviews

## What we build for electrical contractors

- **Campaigns by job type.** Separate search campaigns for panel and service upgrades, EV chargers, generators and general service, each with its own keywords and landing page. Higher-margin campaigns get more budget.
- **Commercial search campaigns** if you want that work, pointing to a commercial page that talks about bids, scheduling around business hours and the types of buildings you handle.
- **Local Services Ads.** As of 2026, Google's verification for electricians in Local Services Ads can include license and insurance checks, depending on location, and it charges per lead rather than per click. We handle setup, service selection and lead review. Read more in [Google Local Services Ads vs. Google Ads](/blog/google-local-services-ads-vs-google-ads/).
- **Event-driven pushes.** Generator ads that can be turned up after major outages, and EV charger campaigns that can sit alongside the rest year-round.
- **Google Business Profile and review work.** Services listed individually, real job photos (clean panel work sells), service area set correctly and a steady review request process.
- **Meta campaigns for considered purchases** like generators or EV chargers aimed at homeowners in your area.
- **Reporting tied to signed jobs**, by campaign and job type.

1. **Audit.** We review your ad accounts, profile and website to see where the budget is going now.

## What to look for (even if you don't hire us)

Run through this list with your current agency or on your own:

- **Does every high-margin service have its own campaign and landing page?**
- **Can you see the search terms?** Watch for "electrician school," "electrical supply store" or DIY wiring searches.
- **Is geographic targeting limited to towns you'll actually serve?**
- **Are phone calls tracked?** Most residential electrical leads call rather than fill out forms.
- **Do you get cost per signed job by type?** Clicks and impressions don't pay anyone.
- **Do you own the ad accounts and the data?**
