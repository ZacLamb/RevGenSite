## The problem in landscaping

Landscaping has a phone problem that is almost entirely about timing. From March into May, every homeowner in your area wakes up wanting a cleanup, mulch and a mowing quote at the same moment, and they call between 8 and 5, exactly when your owner and crew leads are on job sites with gloves on and engines running. Voicemails stack up. By the time someone calls back at 7 p.m., a good share of those people have booked whoever answered.

The rest of the year brings its own repetitive work. Customers ask when the crew is coming. Rain pushes a whole route back a day and somebody has to tell forty customers. Fall cleanup slots need to be sold in September. Past clients from last spring never get asked back because nobody had time to go through the list. Reviews don't get requested because by the time the job is done, the crew is already at the next property.

None of this is hard work. It is just constant, and it falls on the owner or one office person who is also doing estimates, payroll and ordering.

- **Missed-call text back** so anyone who hits voicemail gets a text within moments asking what they need.
- **Seasonal re-booking campaigns.** Last year's cleanup, aeration and leaf customers get an offer for this year's service at the right time. See our guide to [reactivating past customers by text](/blog/reactivate-past-customers-text-campaign/).
- **Weather and schedule notices.** Quick ways to send "crew is coming tomorrow" and "pushed for rain" texts to everyone on a route day.
- **Review requests after completed installs and cleanups,** sent the same day the job is marked done.
- **Quote and proposal automation** that drafts a maintenance proposal from the property details your estimator enters, ready for review and sending.

## How it works

1. **Audit.** We look at where your time goes in peak season: unanswered calls, callbacks, re-booking, reminders, and the questions customers ask over and over.
2. **Design.** We script the AI agent and lay out each automation around how your company actually schedules and sells.
3. **Build and connect.** We connect it to your CRM, phone line and calendar so bookings and notes land where your team already works.
4. **Tune and support.** We monitor it closely for the first 60 days, adjust scripts and timing, and keep tuning as the seasons change.

## What to look for (even if you don't hire us)

- Can the AI agent tell a caller whether you service their town, and politely decline if you don't?
- Does it handle design inquiries differently from mowing quotes, booking a consultation rather than just taking a message?
- What happens when it gets a question it can't answer? There should be a clear handoff to a person.
- Can you send a schedule-change text to a whole route day in a couple of taps?
- Are re-booking messages sent from a registered texting number? As of 2026, US business texting generally requires A2P 10DLC registration.
- Can you listen to call recordings and read transcripts to check what the agent said?
